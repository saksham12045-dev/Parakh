Regulatory framework

The rules behind the screening.

Parakh's screening workflow is framed around the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. The official source remains the authority for the applicable legal text.

LMGovernment reference

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

The official 2011 rules document is provided here as the regulatory reference for package screening. Parakh is intended as a screening and inspection-assistance interface; it does not replace the applicable law or an authorised officer's legal determination.

About Parakh

A practical inspection layer between product images and compliance review.

Parakh is an intelligent inspection layer that converts product package images into structured declarations and compliance insights. It helps identify relevant information quickly and supports transparent, evidence-based regulatory review.
